# Verbal consent from a family member

Read this script to {{full_name}} in the language they know best, then record their answer.

Script
"{{agent_name}} wants to use a computer program to find where your name, phone number and address appear on people-search sites, data brokers, caller-ID apps and search results, and to ask those sites to remove them. It will search only for you. Your details stay on {{agent_name}}'s computer, and the coding agent and its service provider will see them while it works. {{agent_name}} will approve every removal request before it is sent. Some sites may email you, call you or ask for proof of identity. You can say stop at any time. Do you agree?"
