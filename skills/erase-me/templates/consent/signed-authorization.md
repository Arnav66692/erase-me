# Authorization to act as my agent for personal data removal

I, {{full_name}}, authorize {{agent_name}} to act as my agent for the purposes below. I am {{agent_name}}'s {{agent_relationship}}.

Scope
My agent may, on my behalf:
1. Search people-search sites, data brokers, caller-ID apps, phone directories and search engines for my personal data.
2. Submit opt-out, removal and delisting requests to those services.
3. Send requests to delete, erase or stop processing my personal data under any privacy law that protects me, and send follow-ups.
4. File complaints with a data protection regulator about requests that go unanswered.
5. Receive replies about these requests.

Limits
My agent may not:
1. Open, use or change my financial, email or social media accounts.
2. Pay any money in my name.
3. Request removal of public records held by a court or government.
4. Share my personal data with anyone except the service that holds it and, for complaints, the regulator.

Identity checks
Some laws let a business confirm my identity with me directly. If a business contacts me to confirm this authorization, I will answer it.

Duration and revocation
This authorization starts on the date I sign it and lasts until {{agent_name}} has finished these requests or until I revoke it, whichever comes first. I can revoke it at any time by telling {{agent_name}} in writing, including by text message or email. After I revoke it, my agent will send no new requests and will tell services with open requests that the authorization has ended.
