# Competition fit

Research checked November 5, 2026. No entry, payment or competition build was made.
No evidence supports calling a prestigious competition an easy win. Erase Me still
needs a consented, independently verified deletion to substantiate its strongest claim.

| Event | Verified timing | Fit and constraints |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Atlassian Codegeist 2026 | Online; deadline December 22, 2026, noon PST | Established global developer hackathon, free entry and solo entries allowed. Existing projects can qualify with substantive new or rebuilt Forge work during the event. The current app is not eligible unchanged. |
| University of Delaware Diamond Challenge 2027 | Deadline January 14, 2027, 2 p.m. PST | Existing social-impact ventures fit better, but this is a venture competition, not a hackathon. Needs 2–4 high-school students aged 14–18 and an advisor aged 21+. Finalists have an in-person attendance requirement. Entry-fee and travel funding were not verified. |

For a genuine hackathon, **Codegeist is the strongest verified candidate**, conditional on
a useful Forge integration. A privacy-case workflow in Jira or Jira Service Management is
one possible fit, not an organizer endorsement. It would need genuine sponsor functionality,
a working demo and judge installation access. A wrapper around the current website is
unlikely to satisfy the non-trivial implementation requirement.
[Official Codegeist rules](https://codegeist2026.devpost.com/rules).

Codegeist's free entry does not make its infrastructure unlimited or billing-free. Forge
has free allowances but requires billing details and charges usage above them. Avoid a
platform migration or paid service solely to chase a prize without a separate decision.
[Official Forge pricing](https://developer.atlassian.com/platform/forge/forge-platform-pricing/).

A previous first-place Codegeist project, Secure Notes for Jira, demonstrates that privacy
and security products can fit; it does not establish Erase Me's odds of winning.
[Prior winner](https://devpost.com/software/secure-notes-for-jira-coegvb).

Diamond Challenge is the closer existing-product option if the team and advisor rules can
be met. Assess its Social Innovation track and practical attendance costs before entering.
[Official competition page](https://diamondchallenge.org/competition/).

No current 2026 Chrome Built-in AI Challenge was verified. Its 2025 rules and deadline must
not be advertised as a new competition. Sponsor and prior-work rules should be rechecked
immediately before any submission.
