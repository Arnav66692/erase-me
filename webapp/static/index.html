How it works03 / Erased

A smaller footprint.
One site at a time.

You choose when to begin. Broker verification stays with you. Nothing comes back to run in the background.

  1. Find

    Search for your listings

    Open the broker’s search and check that the listing is yours. Use your name together with an age or location before requesting removal.

  2. Request

    Ask each site to remove them

    Open the official form. Supported routes can use the browser companion to fill required fields. Complete any broker email or verification step yourself.

  3. Verify

    Check that the page is gone

    Save the broker’s response and recheck the original listing. Your report stays distinct from an independently verified removal.

        Your information

        Where your data goes.

        The public workspace stores your profile and request history in this browser. Your details are not sent to the site’s server. Other people using this browser profile may be able to read them.

        Erase Me does not connect to your personal inbox. A broker form receives the fields you approve. Complete broker verification emails yourself unless you explicitly use the separate removal-only mailbox pilot. That gated pilot lets its operator check only correlated broker receipts, on demand.

        Your workspace does not run scheduled checks. You can export your record or delete it from this browser. Deleting the workspace does not recall information or requests already sent to brokers.
