        Your information

        Where your data goes.

        Your profile and request history are stored on the computer running this app. If this app is hosted, that means the service’s server.

        The local app has no inbox access. Supported broker forms receive the details needed for your request. Brokers may send verification emails directly to the address you enter, and you complete those steps yourself.

        Demo mode uses synthetic listings and does not send removal requests. You can pause future work or delete your account and its stored history. Deleting your account does not recall requests already sent to other sites.
