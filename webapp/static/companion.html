Return to your workspace

Your browser.
Your requests.

The free companion fills reviewed broker forms in desktop Chrome. Your profile stays in your workspace. Broker forms receive only the fields required for the action you start.

Download companion
  1. Extract the ZIP into a folder you will keep.
  2. Open Chrome’s extensions page at chrome://extensions. Turn on Developer mode.
  3. Choose Load unpacked, then select the extracted folder containing manifest.json.
  4. Open the companion’s toolbar icon. Choose Enable broker forms and accept access to the six listed sites.
  5. Refresh Erase Me. Choose Check companion, then start a broker action from your route list.
  6. Updating an older companion? Replace its extracted files with this download, then choose Reload on Chrome’s extensions page. The current companion is version 0.3.0.

What it does

People Data Labs. Starts the official deletion portal’s email verification, then you open the broker email and finish the steps.

USPhonebook, SearchPeopleFree and FastPeopleSearch. Fills the first official form. Review and submit on the broker page, complete any challenge, then finish the emailed record-selection form.

Apollo. Fills a business email that you enter and authorize separately for your own record. Your profile email is never substituted. Submit and complete verification directly with Apollo.

Spokeo. Fills your email and an exact direct listing URL after you confirm that the listing is yours. Submit on Spokeo and complete its confirmation email.

The optional operator pilot can continue from an authenticated USPhonebook email to its reviewed confirmation form. Review your city, state and ZIP in Erase Me, then choose Continue broker form. The companion fills the authorized name, city, state, ZIP and contact fields. USPhonebook also requires either date of birth or street address. Enter one directly on the official broker form; Erase Me does not collect or autofill those details. A prepared form does not establish removal.

Find, hold and check

Find my listing opens a Google search only when you choose it. Google receives your name and optional city. Review every candidate yourself; a search does not confirm a match.

Hold this route stops its new form, search and mailbox actions. Resume this route releases that hold explicitly, while keeping the references that prevent duplicate actions. Holds survive a private backup and campaign pause/resume.

After a supported submission, Check existing broker tab can read a fresh verification acknowledgment from the original tab without resending the form. This is supported only for People Data Labs and USPhonebook’s first stage, with the companion’s original submission proof. Closed or changed tabs, lost browser-session proof and unreviewed outcomes remain unknown. Verification initiation is not a completed removal.

What remains yours

CAPTCHAs, accuracy agreements, final submissions and matching the correct record. Other verification emails remain your responsibility. SearchPeopleFree uses your typed email; a new shared service email is not offered. The companion does not read mail, upload ID, bypass managed challenges or run in the background on a schedule. An interrupted action is held rather than repeated.

Mobile browsers can use the workspace and official broker links. Companion form filling requires desktop Chrome.

Clearing site data removes your workspace. Export a private backup first. Removing the extension deletes its local operation references and owned-tab metadata. These contain route outcomes, not your profile fields. Browser-session observation proof expires with that browser session.
