The free companion fills reviewed broker forms in desktop Chrome. Your profile stays in your workspace. Broker forms receive only the fields required for the action you start.

Download companion
  1. Extract the ZIP into a folder you will keep.
  2. Open Chrome’s extensions page at chrome://extensions. Turn on Developer mode.
  3. Choose Load unpacked, then select the extracted folder containing manifest.json.
  4. Open the companion’s toolbar icon. Choose Enable broker forms and accept access to the four listed sites.
  5. Refresh Erase Me. Choose Check companion, then start a broker action from your route list.
  6. Updating an older companion? Replace its extracted files with this download, then choose Reload on Chrome’s extensions page. The current companion is version 0.2.1.

What it does

People Data Labs. Starts the official deletion portal’s email verification, then you open the broker email and finish the steps.

USPhonebook, SearchPeopleFree and FastPeopleSearch. Fills the first official form. Review and submit on the broker page, complete any challenge, then finish the emailed record-selection form.

The optional operator pilot can continue from an authenticated USPhonebook email to its reviewed confirmation form. Review your city, state and ZIP in Erase Me, then choose Continue broker form. The companion fills the authorized name, city, state, ZIP and contact fields. USPhonebook also requires either date of birth or street address. Enter one directly on the official broker form; Erase Me does not collect or autofill those details. A prepared form does not establish removal.

What remains yours

CAPTCHAs, accuracy agreements, final submissions and matching the correct record. Other verification emails remain your responsibility. The companion does not read mail, upload ID, bypass managed challenges or run in the background on a schedule. An interrupted action is held rather than repeated.

Mobile browsers can use the workspace and official broker links. Companion form filling requires desktop Chrome.

Clearing site data removes your workspace. Export a private backup first. Removing the extension deletes its local operation references. These contain route outcomes, not your profile fields.
